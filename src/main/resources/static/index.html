ASC 606 / IFRS 15 Revenue Recognition Compliance Note

Under ASC 606 5-Step Model, revenue recognition reflects the transfer of promised goods or services to customers in an amount that reflects the consideration to which the entity expects to be entitled. For this contract, performance obligations and earned revenue schedules are governed by its contractual pricing model.

Balance Sheet Accounting Position

  • Contract Liability (Deferred Revenue): Billed Amount > Earned Revenue
